Eon Commando

Six eras. One commando. No mercy.

A Contra-style run-and-gun that ships as a single self-contained HTML file — six eras, five weapons, nine enemy archetypes, three bosses, a story with in-engine cutscenes, a power gauge, endless waves and local co-op. No install. No dependencies. No network calls.

real frames · gameplay, a boss, and co-op

Six eras, five checkpoints each

I

Ashen Dawn

A burning city street under a dying sun. Ember-red troopers, snipers on the rooftops, and a ceiling that starts falling on you the moment the Warden wakes up.

boss · THE WARDEN — ceiling debris, weak-point core
II

Glacier Eon

A frozen vault of broken ice shelves and long drops. Frost-blue armour, brutes that charge without warning, and a wraith that calls for help.

boss · GLACIER WRAITH — ice fans, swarm summons
III

Eon Core

The heart of time, and it is awake. Shielded troopers you cannot shoot from the front, and a core that sweeps the arena with rotating beams.

boss · THE EON HEART — rotating beams, spiral volleys
IV

Sunken Relay

A relay station that drowned mid-signal. Brutes and shielders hold the flooded decks, and the message it was carrying is addressed to you.

boss · THE DROWNED RELAY — sweeping beam, shielder summons
V

Orbital Wound

The orbit broke here and the sky is still coming down. Nothing underfoot agrees on which way is down.

boss · ORBIT WRECK — circles the arena, drops mines
VI

The Last Second

The end of the clock, in gold and ash. Every enemy type at once, and a Warden that has already watched you win.

boss · THE LAST SECOND — hands that fire as they sweep

What is in the file

Five weapons

Each with its own arc, cadence and power-gauge behaviour.

Nine enemy archetypes

Troopers, snipers, brutes, fliers, turrets and the things that summon.

Weather

Rain and snow that change how a stage reads, not just how it looks.

Local co-op

Two players on one keyboard. No lobby, no connection, no account.

Endless mode

Waves that keep coming after the story is done.

Chiptune

Generated in the engine — no audio files shipped.
