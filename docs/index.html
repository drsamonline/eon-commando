Story and cutscenes

Every mission opens and closes on a line of it.

Six eras, each with an intro and an outro, played in-engine — no video, no images, the same renderer that draws the game draws the scene. A typewriter panel, a portrait plate and a speaker: CONTROL, YOU, and the things you kill. The campaign ends on a credit roll.

cutscene — era I, first line

Power and combo

Kills fill a gauge. The gauge clears the room.

Every kill fills the power bar and extends the combo, and the combo multiplies both your score and the rate the gauge fills. When the bar is full, unleash (<kbd>E</kbd>, or the ⚡ button that appears on a phone): enemy fire is wiped from the screen, everything visible takes damage, you are invulnerable for nearly two seconds and your weapon fires at nearly double rate.

Perks

Every era you hold, you take something with you.

Eight perks, offered three at a time. They stack, they are never offered twice, and they last the whole run — so a run that took Overcharge three times plays nothing like one that took Aegis and Vitality. Damage, rate of fire, health, grenades, power gain, dash recovery, defence and lives.

Ways to play

KEYBOARD · TOUCH · GAMEPAD

Campaign

Three eras end to end, five checkpoints each, a boss at the end of every one. Difficulty tiers from Recruit to Eon.

Endless

The camera locks, the gate shuts, and waves arrive from both sides. A boss every fifth wave, and a score multiplier that climbs 0.15× per wave.

Co-op

Two players, one screen. Shared camera with a soft leash, and going down is not the end — a partner within reach brings you back.

05

Built with nothing

NO ASSETS · NO FONTS · NO CDN

Art at runtime

Every sprite is drawn from paths — rounded caps, arcs, bezier limbs — and every character is reskinned per era by swapping a palette. No image files at all.

Audio at runtime

Two pulse leads, a triangle bass and a noise kit, scheduled through Web Audio. No audio files either.

Deterministic

Seeded simulation, fixed 60 Hz step. A 900-frame replay compresses to 196 characters and reproduces the run exactly.
